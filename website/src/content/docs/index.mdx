<h2 class="vc-section">Settings, without the desktop environment</h2>
<p class="vc-section-lead">The settings app GNOME and KDE users take for granted, for the desktops that leave it to you.</p>

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Network" icon="rocket">
    Wi-Fi, wired and VPNs: connect in a keystroke, and edit every setting, from WPA Enterprise to IPv6, DNS and routes. WireGuard, OpenVPN and Cisco AnyConnect with web sign-in, a hotspot, passwords in your keyring. [Network](/guide/network/)
  </Card>
  <Card title="Bluetooth" icon="setting">
    Pairing with every kind of code, battery levels, headset audio, sending and receiving files, and a phone's internet. [Bluetooth](/guide/bluetooth/)
  </Card>
  <Card title="Displays" icon="laptop">
    Drag monitors into place, set modes, scale, HDR and VRR, and keep it or it goes back by itself. Workspaces on monitors, and profiles that switch when you plug in. [Displays](/guide/displays/)
  </Card>
  <Card title="Sound" icon="seti:audio">
    The output and input in use, a volume per app and where it plays, ports, balance and sound cards' profiles, live with PipeWire. [Sound](/guide/sound/)
  </Card>
  <Card title="Input" icon="seti:config">
    Keyboard layouts, Caps Lock and the Compose key, mice and touchpads, and Hyprland's shortcuts in words, changed in a dialog. [Input](/guide/input/)
  </Card>
  <Card title="Wallpaper" icon="seti:image">
    Pictures from a folder on each monitor, with awww or hyprpaper: transitions, a slideshow, and a command after each change. [Wallpaper](/guide/wallpaper/)
  </Card>
  <Card title="Apps and default apps" icon="window">
    What your launcher shows and how each app starts, edited as your own copy; AppImages and websites as apps; which app opens links and files. [Apps](/guide/apps/) · [Default apps](/guide/default-apps/)
  </Card>
  <Card title="System" icon="information">
    Hardware, storage and distribution, package updates for pacman, the AUR, DNF, zypper, APT, XBPS, Flatpak and Snap, and renaming the computer. [System](/guide/system/)
  </Card>
  <Card title="Your wallpaper's colours" icon="moon">
    Takes its accent from the colours matugen makes from your wallpaper, ML4W's included, and follows each change. Five built-in themes otherwise, dark and light. [Settings](/guide/settings/)
  </Card>
  <Card title="Made for Hyprland" icon="approve-check">
    A normal window with the class <code>ch.giosuel.Lituus</code> for window rules, keyboard first (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> finds any page, setting or action), with a tray icon and quick popup. Its own Hyprland file, never yours. [The window](/guide/window/)
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

<h2 class="vc-section">Up and running in minutes</h2>
<p class="vc-section-lead">It uses what your system most likely runs already: NetworkManager, BlueZ, PipeWire and Hyprland.</p>

<div class="vc-steps">
  <div class="vc-step">
    <span class="vc-step__n">1</span>
    <h3>Install it</h3>
    <p>From the AUR, as a <code>.deb</code> or <code>.rpm</code>, an AppImage, a Flatpak or a Nix flake, for x86-64 and ARM64. See <a href="/install/">Installing</a>.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="vc-step">
    <span class="vc-step__n">2</span>
    <h3>Float it</h3>
    <p>Add a window rule and a keybind, so it opens where you want it. See <a href="/guide/hyprland/">Hyprland</a>.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="vc-step">
    <span class="vc-step__n">3</span>
    <h3>Set it up</h3>
    <p>Pick a network, pair your headphones, place your monitors. Lituus picks up what you already have; for the pages that write to Hyprland, <strong>Set up…</strong> adds one line to your config (see <a href="/guide/hyprland/#lituuss-own-file">Hyprland</a>).</p>
  </div>
</div>
